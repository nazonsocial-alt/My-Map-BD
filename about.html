About

MapMyBangladesh helps you remember and celebrate every district you explore. Your map, notes and dates are stored only in your browser — no accounts, no tracking.

The map shows the real boundaries of all 64 districts, built from geoBoundaries ADM3 data (CC BY 4.0; underlying source Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics / OCHA ROAP), dissolved into districts and simplified for fast loading.

Built with HTML, CSS, vanilla JavaScript and SVG. Image/PDF export loads html2canvas and jsPDF on demand.
